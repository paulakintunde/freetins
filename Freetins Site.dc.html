Cheats are not codes

A code is issued by the developer and expires. A cheat is a button sequence or console command that has been in the game since it shipped. It cannot expire, but it can be patched out, and on most games turning one on disables trophies and achievements for that save. Both facts are on every cheat page.

Every mutation in Grow a Garden and what triggers it

Twenty-three mutations, what causes each one, and what they multiply your sale price by. Everything below was triggered on a test farm rather than copied from a wiki, and the four we could not reproduce are named at the bottom.

Written by Priya Raman, Roblox codes editor
Verified by Marcus Bell · 8 min read · rewritten for the 18 August patch
Updated 3 d ago
Here for the codes instead? Six are live right now, checked 4 minutes ago.
On this page
{{ t.label }}
Tested on a level 42 farm across four weather events between 18 and 20 August 2026.
A Shocked, Wet, Pollinated candy blossom. Three mutations stacked is where the numbers stop being small.

What a mutation actually is

A mutation is a state a plant picks up before you harvest it, and it multiplies the sale price of that plant only. It is applied to the crop, not to the seed, so nothing you do at planting time decides it.

Two things cause them: weather events that sweep the whole farm, and pets that walk the rows and mutate individual plants. Weather is the reason a five-minute window can be worth more than an hour of farming.

The full list

Multipliers are applied to base sale value. Where two mutations combine into a third, the combined one replaces both rather than stacking with them.

Mutation Trigger Value Stacks with
{{ m.n }} {{ m.trigger }} {{ m.mult }} {{ m.stacks }}

How mutations stack

Three rules cover every case we could produce. They are worth knowing because two of them lose you money if you get the order wrong.

  1. Multipliers add, they do not compound. Wet at ×2 and Pollinated at ×3 is ×5 on the same plant, not ×6.
  2. Combination mutations consume their parents. Wet plus Chilled becomes Frozen at ×10, and both parent mutations disappear from the plant.
  3. Rainbow overwrites Golden. If a plant rolls Rainbow at harvest, an existing Golden is gone rather than added, so there is no point holding a Golden crop hoping to double up.

Weather, and what to do when it starts

Weather is announced in chat before it lands, which gives you roughly forty seconds. Do not harvest during an event. Anything still in the ground gets the mutation and anything already in your backpack does not.

Pets that mutate for you

Pet mutations are slow and constant, which makes them the only reliable income when no weather is running. A bee working a full plot pays for itself inside a day.

How a value gets set

An observed value is the median of every completed trade we watched for that item in the last seven days, with the top and bottom tenth thrown away. Nothing is published on fewer than five observations, which is why two rows above say so instead of guessing.

A submitted value is a reader’s screenshot of a completed trade, checked by an editor against the going rate before it goes in. It is labelled differently because it is one trade, not a sample.
