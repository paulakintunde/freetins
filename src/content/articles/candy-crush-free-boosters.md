**Candy Crush Saga does not currently expose a public promo-code box.** Free boosters and lives come through the account's in-game features, rotating events, friends and official offers. Availability can differ by region, app version and account.

The only genuine codes King issues are **paid gift card codes**, and they are not entered in the game at all. You redeem them at **store.king.com** with a code and a PIN, then choose which King game receives the Gold Bars.

## Are there Candy Crush Saga redeem codes for free boosters?

No public free-booster redemption field is documented in the current game or King support material. Paid King gift cards use a separate web redemption flow and should not be confused with in-game promo codes.

This matters because dozens of sites publish "Candy Crush codes August 2026" lists with strings like `CANDY500GOLD`. Those strings are invented, and there is nowhere to type them.

What King does operate:

- **King gift cards** (digital or physical). Bought with money, redeemed at store.king.com using a code plus a PIN, credited as Gold Bars.
- **King Community contests.** Genuine giveaways of Gold Bars and boosters, run in the Contests section of community.king.com.
- **Official social giveaways.** King's cross-checked Candy Crush accounts occasionally post real reward links, usually tied to an event or anniversary.

Treat an unsupported string or generator claim cautiously, especially when it asks for a password, payment details or an app download. ## How does the Daily Treat Machine work?

The Daily Treat Machine opens automatically when you launch the game. It is a login streak meter split into **six tiers, each tier being a 5-day streak**. The higher the tier, the bigger the bonus reward on top of the daily prize.

Hit the **30-day maximum streak** and you get a shot at the jackpot alongside your normal daily reward.

Miss a day and the machine resets you to the previous tier. King's own support wording is blunt about the worst case: it "can also reset all the way to day 1".

Two practical rules:

1. Open the game every single day, even if you do not play a level. The claim takes about five seconds.
2. Keep the app updated. The Treat Machine only appears on the latest build, which is the single most common reason players say it has "disappeared".

## How do you get free lives in Candy Crush Saga?

You start with **5 lives**. Each failed level costs one. A new life regenerates every **30 minutes** until the meter is back to 5, and that costs nothing.

The faster route is other people. Sending a life to a friend does not use one of yours, so the life economy is genuinely free on both sides.

### How to ask friends for lives (iOS and Android)

1. Tap the **heart icon** at the top of the level map screen.
2. Tap **Ask Friends**.
3. Select the friends you want to request from, or tap select all.
4. Tap **Send** to fire off the requests.
5. Accept incoming lives from the same heart menu or from your in-game inbox.

### How to send lives back (iOS and Android)

1. Open the **heart icon** or your message inbox on the map screen.
2. Find the pending requests from friends.
3. Tap **Send** next to each name, or **Send All**.
4. Confirm. Your own life count is untouched.

### How to link Facebook so friends actually appear

Requests go nowhere if your account is not connected to a friends list.

1. Open Candy Crush Saga and tap the **Settings** cog on the map screen.
2. Tap **Connect** and choose **Facebook**.
3. Sign in and approve the permissions prompt.
4. If you already had a separate King account with progress on it, use King's account merge process rather than playing on both. Merging preserves the further progressed save.

### How to farm a friends list without spamming people you know

If your real friends list is empty, that is the actual bottleneck, not the game.

1. Create the King account first, then connect Facebook so both are linked to the same save.
2. Join the Candy Crush groups on King Community and Facebook where players swap add requests. These are the same places King runs its official contests.
3. Add players who are actively posting, not dormant accounts. A dormant friend never sends a life.
4. Send lives out first. Players reciprocate far more often when you have already sent one.
5. Keep the list wide. Twenty active friends is worth more than a hundred lapsed ones.

## Does the clock-change trick for unlimited lives still work?

**No, and it can lock you out for hours.** This is the trick where you close the game, push your device clock forward a few hours, reopen the game to find refilled lives, then set the clock back.

It worked when life timers were calculated on your device. On a linked King or Facebook account the timer is validated against King's servers, so the extra lives either never appear or vanish on the next sync.

The risk is the part nobody mentions. When your device clock and King's clock disagree, the game can calculate a nonsense wait time. King's own support pages document players seeing waits of **hundreds or thousands of minutes** for the next life, and the standard fix is to set your device back to automatic date and time and wait it out.

Verdict: **do not do this in 2026**. It does not deliver lives on a linked account, and the failure mode is worse than the 30-minute wait you were trying to skip.

## Where do free boosters come from in normal play?

**Event rewards.** Rotating events such as Chocolate Box hand out boosters for completing quests and hitting milestones. Events are the single biggest free booster source in the game, and they cost nothing but the levels you were going to play anyway.

**Level completion and stars.** Beating levels awards Gold Bars into the Piggy Bank, and Hard and Super Hard levels pay out more. Milestone levels drop boosters directly.

**Video ads.** Some accounts get an offer at the fail screen to watch a short video for a free life, extra moves or a booster. This is not a setting you can switch on. Availability depends on your region, your build and King's ad rollout, which is why one player sees it and their friend does not.

**The Piggy Bank.** It fills for free as you play and stops collecting once full. Be clear about what it is though: filling it is free, **opening it is a real money purchase**. The pitch is that the Gold Bars inside are discounted against the store bundles, not that they are free.

**King Community contests.** Real, official and consistently overlooked. The Contests section of community.king.com gives away Gold Bars and boosters, and entry is usually a comment or a screenshot.

## Why is my free booster or life not showing up?

In order of how often it actually happens:

1. **The app is out of date.** The Treat Machine, event tiles and ad offers all require the current build. Update from the App Store or Play Store first, before anything else.
2. **Your account is not linked.** Unlinked saves cannot receive lives from friends and cannot sync rewards across devices. Connect a King account and Facebook.
3. **You are on two saves.** Playing on Facebook on desktop and an unlinked local save on your phone splits your boosters between two accounts. Merge them.
4. **The reward went to a different King game.** Gift card Gold Bars are assigned to a chosen game at store.king.com. Pick Candy Crush Saga, not Soda or Friends.
5. **The event ended.** Event boosters are usually time-limited and expire from your inventory if unused. Check the timer on the event tile.
6. **You are in a region without the ad offer.** Video-for-a-life is not available everywhere. There is no toggle and no workaround.
7. **Your device clock is wrong.** See the clock-change section above. Set date and time back to automatic.

## Which Candy Crush "free booster" sites should you avoid?

| Type of site | What it claims | What actually happens | Risk |
|---|---|---|---|
| Booster or Gold Bar generator | "Enter your username for 999,999 Gold Bars" | Endless human verification loops, surveys, app installs. No currency is ever added. | **Scam.** Wasted time, spam sign-ups, occasional card details harvesting |
| Modded APK ("unlimited boosters") | A patched Android build with everything unlocked | Sideloaded app from an unknown publisher, often repackaged with adware or a credential stealer | **Scam plus malware.** Also a terms of service breach |
| "Unlimited lives" hack tools | A downloadable exe or profile that removes the life timer | Executable of unknown origin, no effect on server-side timers | **Scam plus malware** |
| Code list sites | Publishes fake code strings monthly for ad revenue | Nothing to type them into. Pure page-view farming | **Harmless but useless** |
| Account "top-up" sellers | Sells Gold Bars via your login details | Handing over account credentials to a stranger | **Account theft risk** |

Two hard rules. First, **anything that asks for your King or Facebook password is stealing your account**, without exception. King never asks for your password outside its own login screen. Second, **anything that asks you to sideload an APK is putting an unvetted binary on your phone**, and the fact that it also breaks King's terms is the smaller of your two problems.

Currency and progression in Candy Crush Saga are stored server side. That is the whole reason generators cannot work: there is no local number to edit.
