When a future reward is published here, it needs the destination URL and source record. A copied “free rolls today” button without those records would be less useful than this honest empty state.

## Where official Board Kings rewards appear

The current Google Play listing tells players to follow Board Kings on Facebook and Instagram for news and rewards. The official iOS listing links the same Facebook support presence. Use those publisher-linked profiles rather than a group or page with a similar name.

Board Kings rewards are normally mobile deep links, not typed strings. A valid link should open the installed game and let the current account receive the reward. If a page presents a list of “codes” but Board Kings has no matching code-entry field, treat the strings as unsupported.

## How to claim a Board Kings reward link

1. Update Board Kings from Google Play or the App Store.
2. Open the game and confirm the intended account is active.
3. Open the publisher's social post on that device.
4. Tap the reward URL and allow it to open Board Kings.
5. Wait for the in-game reward confirmation.
6. Check the roll balance before retrying.

A desktop browser cannot hand the reward to a mobile install in the same way. If you discover a publisher post on desktop, reopen the official post on the device rather than sending the deep link through an unknown shortening service.

## Free rolls without a reward link

### Hourly refill

The current official app listing says Board Kings provides free rolls every hour. Follow the meter and capacity shown on your account. If the meter is full, spending a small planned session can restart useful refill time.

Do not rely on a fixed rolls-per-hour number from an old guide. Refill rates, caps and account progression can change.

### Daily events and competitions

The official listing also describes daily events, competitions and prizes. Read the live milestone track before increasing the roll multiplier. The best target is a reachable milestone whose reward improves the next session, not simply the largest prize at the end.

### Sticker sets and board progress

Sticker collections, board completion and minigames can return rewards. Check whether an album or event is close to a real breakpoint before spending a saved roll balance.

### Optional reward offers

Some accounts may see optional ad or promotional rewards. Confirm what the current prompt gives and what action it requires. Close any purchase prompt you did not intend to open.

## A safer roll-spending routine

1. Collect the available refill and official gifts.
2. Read the active event and its next reachable milestone.
3. Use a low multiplier while far from a useful board tile.
4. Raise it only when the possible outcome and remaining roll balance justify the risk.
5. Stop after the planned milestone instead of chasing the next one automatically.

Multipliers increase both the possible return and the speed at which the balance disappears. They do not guarantee a preferred dice result.

## Coins, upgrades and visits

Rolls move the piece; Coins fund board upgrades. Holding a large coin balance can expose it to the game's attack and steal systems, so complete a planned upgrade when practical rather than accumulating without a purpose.

When visiting another board, follow the live rules for attacks, police and train movement. An old board layout cannot tell you which tile or protection state the current visit will present.

## Why a Board Kings link fails

| Problem | Likely cause | Safe fix |
|---|---|---|
| Browser opens but game does not | Link opened off-device or app association failed | Reopen on the installed device after updating |
| Game opens with no reward | Expired, already claimed or wrong account | Confirm account and balance, then stop retrying |
| Store page opens | App is missing, outdated or the deep link fell back | Update or install from the official store listing |
| Login, survey or generator appears | Unsupported third-party destination | Close it and use the publisher-linked profile |
| Reward differs from a blog label | The external label was guessed or stale | Trust the in-game confirmation |

Do not share account credentials, install a profile or complete a payment task for promised rolls. Board Kings is free to download but offers real-money purchases and random virtual items, as its store listing states; check platform purchase controls if accidental spending is a concern.

## Make hourly rolls work harder

Let the meter refill while you are away, but avoid leaving it at capacity for long periods when you intend to play. Pair the next session with a reachable event or board-upgrade goal. One controlled session generally preserves more options than rolling until the balance reaches zero because a bonus banner is still visible.
