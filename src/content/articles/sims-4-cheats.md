Press **Ctrl + Shift + C**, type **testingcheats true**, press Enter. Then **motherlode** gives you 50,000 Simoleons, **kaching** or **rosebud** gives 1,000 each, and **money 1000000** sets your household funds to exactly that number. Every other cheat on this page needs testingcheats on first.

The three money cheats and the build cheats work without testingcheats. Everything involving skills, careers, needs, relationships and shift-clicking does not.

## Sims 4 money cheats

Type these in Live Mode with the cheat console open. Repeat them as many times as you like.

| Cheat | What it does | testingcheats needed? | Status |
|---|---|---|---|
| `motherlode` | Adds **50,000** Simoleons | No | Working |
| `kaching` | Adds **1,000** Simoleons | No | Working |
| `rosebud` | Adds **1,000** Simoleons (identical to kaching) | No | Working |
| `money 1000000` | **Sets** funds to the exact number you type | Yes | Working |
| `household.autopay_bills true` | Bills pay themselves, forever | Yes | Working |
| `freerealestate on` | All lots cost **0** when you move in | No | Working, see note |
| `sims.modify_funds 5000` | Adds the number you type to funds | Yes | Working |

**Note on freerealestate:** it only works from the world map or lot selection screen while you are moving a household in. Typing it during normal Live Mode does nothing, which is a common reason players report it as broken.

`money` is a set, not an add. `money 0` will empty your household.

## How to open the cheat console on PC, Mac, PS5 and Xbox

The console is the text bar that appears at the top of the screen. Cheats typed anywhere else, including the build mode search box, will not fire.

### PC (Windows)

1. Load into a household in **Live Mode**.
2. Press **Ctrl + Shift + C** together.
3. The cheat bar appears at the top of the screen.
4. Type `testingcheats true` and press **Enter**.
5. Type your cheat and press **Enter**.
6. Press **Ctrl + Shift + C** again, or **Esc**, to close the bar.

### Mac

1. Load into Live Mode.
2. Press **Command + Shift + C**. On some keyboards **Ctrl + Shift + C** also works.
3. If neither fires, hold **fn** as well: **fn + Command + Shift + C**.
4. Type `testingcheats true`, press **Enter**, then enter your cheat.

### PlayStation 5 and PlayStation 4

1. Load into Live Mode.
2. Hold **L1 + L2 + R1 + R2** at the same time. All four shoulder buttons, held for about a second.
3. The cheat bar opens with an on-screen keyboard.
4. Type `testingcheats true` and confirm.
5. Enter your cheat and confirm again.

A USB or Bluetooth keyboard plugged into a PS5 also responds to **Ctrl + Shift + C**.

### Xbox Series X|S and Xbox One

1. Load into Live Mode.
2. Hold **LB + RB + LT + RT** at the same time.
3. The cheat bar opens with an on-screen keyboard.
4. Type `testingcheats true`, confirm, then enter your cheat.

A USB keyboard on Xbox also responds to **Ctrl + Shift + C**.

**On both consoles**, enabling testingcheats pops a warning that achievements and trophies will be turned off. That warning is real. See the achievements section below.

**How to use cas.fulleditmode properly**, because typing it alone does nothing visible:

1. Open the console, type `testingcheats true`, press Enter.
2. Type `cas.fulleditmode`, press Enter.
3. Hold **Shift** and click the Sim you want to edit.
4. Choose **Modify in CAS**.
5. Everything is now unlocked, including traits and age.

The cheat resets when you exit CAS. Re-enter it each time.

**UI Cheats Extension** is a script mod by weerbesu, not a built-in cheat. It lets you click money and need bars directly instead of typing. ## Do Sims 4 cheats disable achievements in 2026?

**Yes, but only cheats that require testingcheats, and only in the save file you used them in.** EA's own cheats FAQ states that the codes needing `testingcheats true` are the ones that disable achievements in your current game save, and that other saves are unaffected.

That means:

- `motherlode`, `kaching`, `rosebud`, `freerealestate` and `bb.moveobjects` are **achievement-safe**. EA has specifically named `bb.moveobjects` and `bb.showliveeditobjects` as safe.
- `testingcheats true` and anything downstream of it (skills, careers, needs, relationships, cas.fulleditmode) **switches achievements off for that save**.
- Start a new save and achievements are live again.

**One thing to be careful about:** some long-standing forum answers claim achievements are only disabled on consoles and that PC and Mac players keep theirs. EA's guidance and platform behavior are not described consistently in the reviewed references. If achievements matter to you, keep a separate uncheated save and accept the warning shown by your platform.

## Why is my Sims 4 cheat not working?

In the order these actually happen:

**1. You skipped testingcheats.** Roughly every skill, career, needs and shift-click cheat needs `testingcheats true` typed first, in that session. It does not persist through a game restart. Retype it every time you load.

**2. Shift-click does nothing.** Same cause. Shift-click menus only appear after testingcheats is on. On a Mac laptop, use the left Shift key, and if you are on a trackpad use two-finger click while holding Shift.

**3. The console will not open on PC.** Something else has claimed Ctrl + Shift + C. The usual culprits are Gyazo, ShareX, AMD ReLive and other screenshot or capture overlays. Close them, or rebind their hotkey. Alternatives that work: **Ctrl + Shift + Windows key + C**. Also make sure the game window is actually focused if you play windowed, and that your cursor is not sitting in the Build Mode search box.

**4. Still will not open.** Close the game, go to `Documents\Electronic Arts\The Sims 4\`, move **Options.ini** to your desktop, and relaunch. The game rebuilds it. You will lose your graphics settings, not your saves.

**5. Cheats stopped working right after a patch.** Patch 1.126 landed on 21 July 2026 and, as every major patch does, it switched off **Script Mods Allowed**. Script mods such as MC Command Center and UI Cheats Extension will be inactive and out-of-date ones can block the console entirely. Go to Game Options > Other, retick both mod boxes, restart, and update or remove any mod that has not been patched for 1.126.

**6. The cheat runs but nothing happens.** You do not own the pack that skill or career belongs to, or the ID has a typo. IDs are not case sensitive but they are spelling sensitive. `Major_Cooking` does nothing. `Major_HomestyleCooking` works.

**7. freerealestate says nothing.** It only works from the world map or the lot picker while moving a household in, not from Live Mode.

**8. You typed it in the wrong box.** In Build and Buy Mode the search field looks similar to the cheat bar and truncates long input. Switch to Live Mode, open the console with the key combo, and check the bar is at the top of the screen.
