Brookhaven RP lets you build a roleplay session around a home, an avatar and the tools already available in the game. You do not need a redeem code to begin. The useful distinction is between a free selection and a feature whose menu asks you to buy a pass.

## Claim a free house first

Open the house controls from the right sidebar, choose a vacant property and select a house you can use. The [publisher's house guide](https://www.brookhavenrp.com/guides/houses) includes free options such as a Tree House, Trailer and Family Suburban home. Choose the layout that supports the scene you want to play rather than assuming the largest visible building is included.

Read the selection screen before confirming. Brookhaven shows properties associated with passes as well as free choices, and seeing a house in the catalog does not establish ownership. A purchase prompt is the point where you should decide whether the extra feature is useful.

The house guide also describes a cooldown when changing homes. Pick a workable layout and spend a little time arranging the roleplay instead of cycling through buildings without a plan.

## Set up the scene with available tools

Start with the avatar and tool menus and choose what your account can use. A backpack item, a job label or a roleplay prop serves a different purpose from a permanent Roblox Marketplace accessory. Using a tool inside Brookhaven does not necessarily add an item to the platform-wide avatar inventory.

For a simple scene, agree on the place and roles with friends first: a family home, a school visit or an emergency call. Then select the tools needed for that activity. This keeps the session useful even when you have no interest in purchasing more vehicles or houses.

If a tool or property is locked, read its label rather than looking for a code that promises to unlock every premium option. The publisher's documentation describes passes as purchases with defined features, not as hidden rewards from a generic string.

## Use house access controls deliberately

Inspect the house's lock and roommate or family controls before inviting people into the scene. The publisher's guide explains access management and notes that some settings reset when a house is changed. Recheck those controls after moving instead of assuming the previous home configuration carried over.

Saving is also a separate action from selecting a house. Use the house menu's save controls for an arrangement you want to keep and read the slot limits. A decoration session should not depend on an assumption that every change is automatically preserved.

## Music IDs are not Brookhaven reward codes

The [official pass guide](https://www.brookhavenrp.com/guides/game-passes) describes Music Unlocked as access to a large song library for the Boombox, houses, cars and other contexts. That is the publisher-supported description. It does not promise that every numeric Roblox audio ID from an outside list can be played in every Brookhaven control.

Our [Roblox song ID guide](/guides/roblox-song-ids/) explains audio identifiers and their limitations. Use it to understand the difference between an audio asset and a promotion, then follow the music interface actually available to your account.

If an ID fails, do not assume that typing it into a different field will grant a free music pass. The ID identifies audio; a redeem code identifies a promotion. Neither label should be silently substituted for the other.

## Decide whether an upgrade solves your problem

The publisher identifies Premium, Land Unlocked, Estates Unlocked and other passes with different feature sets. Compare the named feature with the activity you want to host. A pass that unlocks a special lot does not automatically include every vehicle or every music feature.

Check the current in-game purchase screen for pricing and ownership. This guide avoids a fixed Robux price because a copied amount is less useful than the terms your account is actually being offered. You can keep roleplaying with free selections while deciding whether any paid option adds something you want.
