Vice City cheats work during normal play. PC players type a phrase; console players enter a controller sequence. The useful distinction is not just platform, but edition: Classic and Definitive Edition share most cheats, while Definitive Edition adds a small number of effects and can disable achievements on a cheated save.

Save first. The pedestrian riot and aggression cheats can interfere with mission actors and may remain embedded in a save even after the visible effect appears to stop.

## How to enter Vice City cheats

### PC

Type the code while Tommy is controllable. You do not need to open a console or press Enter. Uppercase and lowercase both work. A confirmation message appears when the code registers.

### PlayStation and Xbox

Press the complete combination during gameplay. Use the d-pad for directions. If Tommy performs normal actions but no confirmation appears, restart the sequence and enter it faster.

### Switch and mobile

Switch requires Switch-labelled face buttons because its `A` and `B` positions differ from Xbox. On supported mobile builds, a physical keyboard may accept the PC phrases. Touch-only controls are not a reliable route to every cheat.

## Before using any Vice City cheat

- Keep a clean manual save outside the cheated session.
- Definitive Edition achievements can be disabled on the affected save.
- Riot and armed-pedestrian effects are not safe mission tools.
- Vehicle spawns need open road and enough room around Tommy.
- There is no legitimate Vice City money cheat.
- Reloading a pre-cheat save is safer than trying to toggle every effect off.

## Why a Vice City cheat is not working

1. Confirm whether you have Classic or Definitive Edition.
2. Leave the pause menu and any text-entry screen.
3. Use the d-pad, not the analog stick.
4. Move away from interiors before spawning a vehicle.
5. Restart the combination from the first input.
6. Reload a clean save if another world-state cheat is active.
