## What “Roblox gear” should mean

Roblox is a platform containing many different experiences, not one game with one control scheme. A branded headset, keyboard or controller does not gain special compatibility merely because Roblox appears on the packaging. Hardware should be chosen for the phone, computer or console and for the experiences a player actually uses.

The Roblox-specific purchase with the clearest function is a gift card. It adds Roblox Credit to the selected account and can then be used for Robux or other services Roblox currently designates. Even here, the seller, delivery type, account and redemption route matter more than the card artwork.

## The safest Roblox gift purchase

Start with Roblox's gift-card page or a retailer Roblox authorizes in the buyer's country. Canadian terms explicitly warn that cards from unauthorized sellers, resellers and auction sites are not valid. A large discount from a social-media account is therefore a risk signal, not a bargain to rush.

Before paying, confirm:

- The card is denominated for the intended country or the current Roblox conversion terms are acceptable.
- The listing is digital or physical, and the delivery time matches the occasion.
- The recipient has access to the Roblox account that should receive the credit.
- The retailer's gift-card return policy is understood; prepaid products are commonly final sale.
- No seller asks for a password, two-step verification code or browser cookie.

For a Canadian denomination, use the full [Roblox $25 CAD gift-card guide](/gear/roblox/roblox-gift-card-25-cad/) before checkout.

## What a gift card adds

Redeeming a standard gift card adds credit to the signed-in Roblox account. That credit can be used for Robux or eligible Roblox services under the terms shown at the time. It is safer to describe the card by its currency value than promise a fixed Robux amount, because pricing, Premium effects and bonus offers can change.

Roblox's Canadian terms currently say cards do not expire and do not carry inactivity, maintenance or service fees. They also say cards are not reloadable, generally cannot be refunded or exchanged for cash, and should not be resold. Review the terms again if buying later; this guide records what was published on its review date rather than turning it into a permanent guarantee.

## Redeem on the intended account

1. Open a browser and sign in to the Roblox account that should own the credit.
2. Visit Roblox.com/redeem directly rather than following a link in a message.
3. Enter the gift-card code and select Redeem.
4. Confirm that credit or the expected item appears before discarding the card or receipt.
5. Keep proof of purchase private until the balance is confirmed.

The standard mobile app and console apps do not provide the normal gift-card redemption route. Roblox currently documents an in-app exception for Samsung Galaxy users, but a browser remains the consistent general method.

## Gift cards, promo codes and toy codes are different

A gift-card code carries paid value. A promotional code may grant a virtual item and can expire. A toy or virtual-item code belongs to the product that supplied it. Entering one type on the wrong page creates an error without showing that the code is fake.

## Hardware for playing Roblox

Buy hardware for the platform, not the logo:

### Mobile

A controller can help only in experiences that support it. Check support in the experience and measure the phone before buying. The [mobile controller guide](/gear/mobile/controller-for-mobile-play/) covers direct USB-C and Bluetooth options.

### PC and Mac

A comfortable mouse, keyboard and headset should use standard connections and software supported by the computer. A one-handed keypad can help some players, but macros may be restricted by an experience's rules. The [PC and console gear guide](/gear/pc-and-console/) starts with compatibility rather than branding.

### Console

Use accessories licensed or explicitly supported for the console. Roblox availability and individual experience features can differ by platform. A product listing that says only “works with Roblox” is not a substitute for a console compatibility statement.

## Merchandise and collectibles

Physical toys and clothing can be enjoyable gifts, but judge them as merchandise. Check age guidance, materials, dimensions, seller identity and whether an advertised virtual item is actually included. Do not value an unverified code more highly than the product itself.

Marketplace descriptions are easy to copy. Prefer the manufacturer or a recognized retailer, and treat “rare code included” as unsupported unless the sealed product and official terms establish it. Never share a code publicly to ask whether it is real; another person can redeem it first.

## Scam signs to stop on

- The seller wants the recipient's Roblox password or security code.
- A “balance checker” asks you to enter the PIN before purchase.
- The price is far below the card's face value without an authorized promotion.
- Delivery moves to direct messages, cryptocurrency or an unrelated payment app.
- The URL imitates Roblox but uses another domain.
- The seller asks for a screen-share session to “activate” the card.

If a card fails, keep the receipt and contact the retailer first for a delivery problem. Roblox support lists the purchase amount, retailer, date, location, PIN, serial number, receipt and error image among the details it may need. Send that information only through the official support route.
