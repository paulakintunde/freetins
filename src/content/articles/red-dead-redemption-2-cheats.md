Red Dead Redemption 2 hides cheats as phrases rather than button combinations. Every platform uses the same words, but several phrases stay locked until Arthur or John owns the correct newspaper issue after the required story mission.

Cheats are a temporary sandbox. Once any cheat is active, saving is disabled and trophies or achievements cannot be earned in that session. Make a manual save before opening the Cheats menu.

## How to enter RDR2 cheats on PC, PlayStation and Xbox

1. Pause the game and select **Settings**.
2. Use the prompt in the lower-right corner to open **Cheats**. Press Triangle on PlayStation or Y on Xbox.
3. Press the same button again to enter a new phrase.
4. Type the phrase. Capitalization does not matter, but words and punctuation should match.
5. Select the unlocked cheat and switch it on.

PS5 and Xbox Series consoles use the same backward-compatible menu as PS4 and Xbox One. PC uses the same phrase list.

## Why newspapers matter

Buying one early newspaper does not unlock every printed phrase. Later issues become available as the story progresses, and the relevant paper must be in the character's inventory. Newspaper sellers operate in Valentine, Strawberry, Rhodes, Saint Denis, Blackwater and Annesburg as their regional editions become available.

If a phrase is correct but remains locked, check three things:

- the required story mission is complete;
- the later newspaper issue has been purchased;
- the paper is still in the satchel.

## Can cheats be saved or used for trophies?

No. Activating a cheat blocks saving and trophy or achievement progress for the current session. The safest workflow is:

1. make a manual save;
2. activate and test cheats;
3. return to the title screen when finished;
4. load the manual save rather than relying on autosave.

Entering a phrase into the list is not the same as switching it on. You can store unlocked phrases in the Cheats menu, but the restriction begins when a cheat is activated.

## Troubleshooting invalid or locked phrases

- Retype curly punctuation as the ordinary keyboard character shown by the game.
- Keep every word, including small words such as `is`, `the` and `of`.
- Buy the newspaper after its associated mission makes that issue available.
- Check the satchel rather than assuming a paper seen at a vendor was purchased.
- If the menu has no entry prompt, back out to Settings and use the lower-right control hint.
- Do not confuse Red Dead Online with Story Mode; this menu is a single-player feature.

Players searching for an online money code should stop there: these cheats do not work in Red Dead Online, and third-party tools risk the account.
