The fastest way to find a Wordscapes answer is to use both the level number and the letters on the wheel. The letter set is a stronger cross-check than a pack label when different app versions or answer sites number a puzzle differently.

The reviewed source draft provides answer sets for **levels 1 to 40** in the tables below. Wordscapes has thousands of levels, so this page states its coverage rather than presenting an incomplete table as a full-game answer sheet.

## The fastest way to solve any Wordscapes level in 20 seconds

This works on every level, at any number, on iOS and Android. It does not need an app, an account or a download.

1. Look at the letter wheel at the bottom of your screen. Write down every letter, including any letter that appears twice.
2. Type those letters into any search box as one string, with no spaces, followed by `wordscapes`. Example: `savedwordscapes`.
3. Compare the result's level number, pack label and letter set with your screen before using its answers.
4. If you would rather not leave the game, use the in-game hint button instead. It costs coins but reveals a letter in place.

There is a second trick that costs nothing at all. **The letter set is always exactly the letters of the longest word on the board.** Count the squares in the longest slot, find a word of that length using every letter once, and the rest of the board is made of the short words hiding inside it. Level 16 gives you A, C, R, T, T. The five-letter slot is TRACT, and ART, CAR, CAT, RAT, TAR and CART all fall out of it.

## How to find your Wordscapes level number

Most people search a pack name because that is what the map screen shows, then land on a page numbered differently. Your actual number is on screen the whole time.

1. Open the puzzle you are stuck on (not the map).
2. Look at the top left of the board. The number printed there, for example **Level 27**, is the game level number.
3. The pack name sits next to it or just above it, for example "Dew 7". Both refer to the same puzzle.
4. If you only have the pack name, use the ranges in the next section to convert it.

## Which Wordscapes levels do people get stuck on, and why

Difficulty in Wordscapes does not track the level number. A level 27 can stop you dead while a level 300 falls in ten seconds. Three things cause almost every stuck point.

**Repeated letters.** The wheel shows the same letter twice and players assume it is a rendering quirk. It is not, and you need both. Level 16 has two Ts (TRACT), level 19 has two Es (GLEE, EAGLE), level 25 has two Ps (APPLY), level 31 has two Ds (DICED), level 33 has two Ts (TENTH) and level 38 has two Ns (NINTH). If a five-letter slot will not fill, count the letters again.

**Obscure or dated words.** The dictionary includes words nobody says out loud. GRAD at level 37, DOLE at level 14, SILO at level 27, TUBER at level 39, BETA at level 11 and BRAN at level 12 all block people who are looking for something more everyday.

**Three-letter fillers.** The board is padded with short words that exist only to link the long ones. ABS, ARC, ION, SOD, SLY and DUO are the ones that stall people. When a long word is already placed and one three-letter slot remains, run the vowels through it: pick the vowel, then try every consonant either side.

**Plurals and near-anagrams.** Levels that want both the singular and the plural, or two anagrams of the same letters, feel like a bug. Level 26 wants PEAK, PEAKS and SPEAK. Level 34 wants SAINT, SATIN and STAIN. Level 21 wants CAB, CABS, CUB and CUBS. If you have found a word and the slot is still empty, add an S or reshuffle the same letters.

## How hints, shuffle and coins work in Wordscapes

**Shuffle is free.** The circular arrow in the centre of the letter wheel reorders the letters and costs nothing. Use it before you spend anything, because most stuck points are a reading problem, not a vocabulary problem.

**Hints cost coins.** The hint buttons above the board spend coins to reveal a letter on the crossword. PeopleFun support confirms that coins are used for hints but does not publish one fixed cost for every account, so check the number on your own button before tapping.

**Bonus words pay you back.** Any valid word you make from the wheel that is not on the crossword goes into the bonus jar and earns coins. This is the single largest free coin source in the game. Before you finish a level, spend thirty seconds spelling every extra word you can see. The level does not end until you complete the crossword, so there is no time pressure.

## What is the Wordscapes daily puzzle and is it worth it?

The daily puzzle is a separate track from your main level number. Solving it does not advance you through the packs, and getting stuck on it does not block your progress.

Each calendar day unlocks one puzzle, and completing it marks that day on the in-game calendar. The rewards are its own, paid on top of anything the main levels give you, which makes it the cheapest routine coin top-up in the game if you open the app anyway.

Two practical notes. The daily puzzle letters are unique like every other level, so the letter search method at the top of this page works on it too. And because it resets on a calendar day, answer pages for "today's" daily puzzle are stale by definition the moment the day rolls over. Check the date stamp on any page claiming to have it.

## Why is my Wordscapes answer not working?

In order of how often it actually happens:

1. **You are on a different level than the page thinks.** This is the big one. Pine 1 is level 13, not level 1, and sites disagree about the early numbering. Compare the letters on your wheel to the letters in the table. If they do not match, the level number is the problem.
2. **You missed a repeated letter.** Two Ts, two Ps, two Ns. Count the wheel, not the word you have in your head.
3. **You typed a bonus word.** The word was valid and paid you coins, but it is not on the crossword. That is working as intended, not a failure.
4. **The app updated and shifted the level.** PeopleFun adds and reorders levels between versions. Older answer lists drift. Letters do not drift.
5. **You are playing a different PeopleFun game.** Wordscapes Uncrossed, Wordscapes Search and Word Stacks share the art style and confuse a lot of searches. The answers do not transfer.
