This page brings the established GTA 5 Story Mode cheats into one platform-labelled sheet, with PC console commands, PlayStation and Xbox button inputs, and phone numbers where that method is supported. Save first: activating a cheat disables trophies and achievements for the current session.

Quick facts before you scroll:

- Cheats work in **Story Mode only**, not GTA Online.
- GTA 5 does not include a built-in Story Mode money cheat.
- Phone-number inputs are for PC and the PS4, PS5, Xbox One and Xbox Series versions. Use button combinations on PS3 and Xbox 360.

## How to Enter GTA 5 Cheats on Each Platform

There are three input methods. All of them must be entered during normal gameplay (not in a menu), and button combos need to be pressed fairly quickly.

### PC: Console Commands

1. Press the tilde key (`~`) during gameplay to open the command console. On most UK and EU keyboards this is the key directly below Esc, sometimes printed as `` ` `` or `¬`.
2. Type the cheat word (for example `PAINKILLER`). It is not case sensitive.
3. Press Enter. A confirmation message appears on screen.

If the console does not open, check your keyboard layout in Windows. On some layouts the console key maps differently; switching to a US layout temporarily is the quickest fix.

### PS3, PS4 and PS5: Button Combos

Press the button sequence on your controller during gameplay. The d-pad directions (Left, Right, Up, Down) are the d-pad, not the analog stick. The combos are identical on PS3, PS4 and PS5, including the GTA V Enhanced version on PS5. There is no separate set of "PS5 cheats"; Rockstar kept the same inputs across every PlayStation generation.

If a combo will not register, you are probably entering it too slowly. You have roughly a second between inputs.

### Xbox 360, Xbox One and Xbox Series X|S: Button Combos

Same rule as PlayStation: press the sequence during gameplay using the d-pad for directions. The combos are identical across Xbox 360, Xbox One and Series X|S, including GTA V Enhanced.

### PC and newer consoles: the in-game cell phone

Phone dialing is available on PC, PS4, PS5, Xbox One and Xbox Series X|S. It is not the input route for the original PS3 and Xbox 360 releases.

1. Press Up on the d-pad (or T on PC keyboard, Up arrow works too) to bring up your character's phone.
2. Open Contacts, then press the button shown to switch to the keypad (X on Xbox, Square on PlayStation, Spacebar on PC).
3. Dial the full number, including the 1-999 prefix, and call it.
4. The cheat activates instantly. The phone also saves dialed numbers, so you can redial recent cheats from the call history.

Every number below follows the same pattern: 1-999 followed by the cheat word spelled out on the keypad. That is a handy way to remember them, and it is how we verify numbers when sources disagree.

## Read This Before You Cheat

- **Cheats disable trophies and achievements for the session.** The block lasts until you reload a save or restart the game. It does not permanently flag your profile.
- **Save first, cheat second.** Cheats cannot be saved with your game, but keeping a dedicated "chaos save" separate from your main story save means you never risk saving over legitimate progress.
- **Cheats never work in GTA Online.** Attempting to use anything cheat-like online is a fast way to get suspended or banned. Story Mode only.
- **Most cheats toggle off** by entering the same code again. The rest expire on their own (invincibility lasts 5 minutes) or clear when you reload a save.
- **Some cheats stack.** Moon gravity plus the Sanchez, or explosive melee plus fast run, are classic combos. Experiment.

## No, There Is No GTA 5 Money Cheat

This is the most searched GTA 5 cheat that does not exist. There is no money cheat in GTA 5 and there never has been, on any platform, in any version. No button combo, no console command, no phone number. Every video or website claiming otherwise is either showing a mod (PC only, single player only) or farming clicks.

Why? Because GTA 5's economy is connected to GTA Online's shark card business and to the single player stock market. A free money code would break both. Rockstar deliberately shipped the game without one.

What actually works instead:

- **The Lester assassination missions plus the stock market.** Invest all three characters' money before each assassination and you can turn the endgame payout into over $2 billion. This is the closest thing to a legitimate money cheat in the game.
- **Random events, armored trucks and store robberies** for early-game cash.
- **In GTA Online**, money comes from heists, businesses and events, and cheating there gets you banned. Our GTA Online money guide covers the fastest legitimate methods that are current in 2026.
