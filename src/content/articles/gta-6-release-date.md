Rockstar's announced GTA 6 release date is November 19, 2026. Digital preload begins November 12. The announced launch platforms are PlayStation 5 and Xbox Series X|S, while Take-Two's preorder announcement lists US prices of $79.99 for Standard and $99.99 for Ultimate. Those are publisher statements, not estimates from a countdown page.

## The release date Rockstar actually lists

The [official GTA VI site](https://www.rockstargames.com/VI) displays November 19, 2026 alongside the console platforms. Use that page when comparing an old delay report with a newer headline. A story describing a previous target can remain accurate as history while no longer describing the announced launch plan.

A release date does not by itself establish a precise unlock hour for every region. This guide does not convert the date into a worldwide midnight promise. Check the relevant platform store for any subsequently published regional unlock details.

## Preload starts earlier than play

The [Rockstar Store](https://store.rockstargames.com/game/buy-gta-vi?drm=PlayStation+5) lists November 12, 2026 for preload. Preloading means downloading the game files before launch; it should not be interpreted as permission to play the story a week early.

Before the download window, confirm which account owns the purchase and which console will receive it. Inspect the platform's storage requirement when it becomes available rather than reserving space based on an unverified file-size claim. The sources reviewed here do not establish a final download size.

The store also describes the physical offering as a code inside a box and explicitly says a disc is not included. Read the exact retailer listing if buying that format. The word physical can describe packaging without establishing disc-based installation.

## Standard and Ultimate prices

[Take-Two's June 24 preorder announcement](https://ir.take2games.com/node/32311/pdf) supplies the announced US prices: $79.99 for the game and $99.99 for Ultimate. Local currencies, tax and storefront terms should be read from the store serving your account rather than calculated from those dollar figures as a promised checkout price.

Ultimate is described as a collection of additional vehicles, weapons, clothing and other story content. Compare its actual included items with your interests before choosing it. The existence of a higher-priced edition does not establish an earlier playable release date.

A preorder bonus and an edition's included content can have separate conditions. Read the purchase screen and the publisher's terms for the exact offer you are considering, particularly if comparing a boxed code with a digital store purchase.

## The announced consoles and the PC question

Rockstar names PS5 and Xbox Series X|S on the official page. No PC release date is established by the sources reviewed for this guide. That is an unanswered scheduling question, not proof that a PC edition can never exist.

The same discipline applies to other platforms. A trailer running on a particular console, an enthusiast prediction or the history of earlier GTA releases does not announce another platform. Look for Rockstar naming it directly.

A preorder announcement confirms purchase and release information. It does not confirm cheat inputs, achievement behavior or how any future online mode will handle them. Those claims need their own evidence.

## What to check before committing your plans

Reopen the publisher page and your platform's store before arranging a download or purchase. Confirm the release date, the product edition and your region's terms. If a publisher update changes one detail, it should replace that detail in this guide without turning every unrelated rumor into confirmed information.
