## How to claim an energy link safely

1. Update Family Island from the official app store.
2. Open the game and confirm the intended account.
3. Find the reward through a publisher-linked social channel.
4. Open the URL on the same device as the game.
5. Allow Family Island to launch.
6. Check the energy balance and any reward message before retrying.

If the link opens the app but nothing changes, restart once and recheck the current account. Do not keep tapping an expired or already-used link, and do not install a “link fixer.”

## Free energy and rewards inside the game

The exact feature set changes with events and account progress, but the useful categories remain consistent.

### Normal progression and island tasks

Follow objectives that unlock production, buildings or the next required area. Clear only the resources required for the current plan. Removing every obstacle on sight can consume energy without advancing a reward track.

### Event milestones and bonus objects

Read the active event's next milestone before spending a large reserve. Collect visible bonus objects and free progress rewards, but compare the energy needed for the next step with the reward actually displayed.

### Food, production and timed systems

Use the current home-island production systems that restore or support energy where available to your account. Keep ingredients moving before an active session so the next refill does not begin from an empty queue.

### Optional ads and store gifts

Some versions or accounts can show optional reward ads or free store items. Confirm that the prompt shows no charge and that the reward is useful. Store layouts and frequency can vary.

### Friend invitations

The official help center documents invitation rewards with eligibility conditions. Open the invitation feature through the game's settings or store area, share the generated game link, and read the current tier terms.

An invite may not qualify if the recipient previously installed the game or does not complete the stated conditions. Do not promise a fixed reward before the live invitation screen confirms it.

## A low-waste energy routine

1. Collect free production and account rewards before clearing objects.
2. Read the current task and identify the exact resources it needs.
3. Queue long production before leaving the game.
4. Spend energy toward one reachable milestone.
5. Stop when the next reward requires a disproportionate or unclear spend.
6. Return after regeneration or production rather than buying impulsively.

An event timer creates urgency, not value. If the next milestone is out of reach with the visible balance and normal refills, preserving energy for the next useful task is a valid choice.

## Why a Family Island link fails

| Problem | Likely cause | Safe fix |
|---|---|---|
| App opens with no energy | Link expired, was already claimed or reached another account | Confirm account and balance, then stop retrying |
| Store opens instead of the game | App is missing, outdated or deep-link handling failed | Update through the official store and reopen |
| Browser cannot continue | Link was opened on a different device | Open the original publisher post on the installed device |
| Reward label does not match | Third-party page guessed or reused a label | Trust the in-game reward confirmation |
| Password, survey or payment requested | Destination is not a normal publisher claim | Close it without providing information |

For account-specific failures, use the help center linked from the official store listing. Include the player identifier and a screenshot of the in-game result when support requests them; do not post private account details publicly.

## No generators or unlimited-energy tools

An external website cannot safely add unlimited energy by asking for a username, human-verification payment or installed profile. Legitimate rewards arrive through Family Island, its publisher channels, official app stores and documented invitation or event systems.

Avoid modified app packages. They can expose the account and device, break updates and invalidate any troubleshooting information the official support team could use.
