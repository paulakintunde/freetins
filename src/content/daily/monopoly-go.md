## How the links actually work and where they originate

{{developer}} posts each link to its own social channels and the link resolves on the mply.io domain. Nothing else mints a valid one. There is no code to type and no account details to hand over: tapping the link credits the rolls to whichever account is signed in on that device.

Reward size varies by drop. Ordinary drops are worth 25 rolls, weekend and event drops run to 75, and cosmetic drops give a shield skin instead of dice. Rolls from a link stack on top of the ones the game hands out through daily bonuses, so a link is extra rather than a substitute for playing.

If you are collecting rolls towards a specific goal, the [Tycoon Club eligibility and benefits](/guides/monopoly-go-tycoon-club/) and the [Golden Blitz trade window](/guides/monopoly-go-golden-blitz/) both pay out more in one run than a single dice link does.

## Why your Monopoly GO dice link is not working

- You already claimed it. Each link is single use per account, and the already claimed message means it worked the first time.
- The window closed. Most drops stop crediting after roughly 3 days.
- You opened it on a desktop. Tap it on the device running the game.
- The drop hit its redemption cap. Some links stop crediting once enough players have used them, before the window is up.
- Your account has not progressed far enough. Several outlets report a level gate, which we could not confirm with the publisher.

## What to do when none are live

Nothing on this page will help you conjure a drop that does not exist, so the honest answer is to use the in game routes instead. Daily bonuses, the free spins on the wheel, sticker pack rewards and tournament placings all pay rolls without a link, and event boards pay considerably more than any single drop.

Then set a check rather than refreshing an aggregator. A drop is live for roughly 3 days, so one look at the official Instagram Story each morning catches almost everything without you watching a feed.
