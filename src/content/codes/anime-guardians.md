## The newest update rewards first

Anime Guardians codes can add Gems, Coins, rerolls and update-specific currencies to an account. The table keeps five recent entries that appear together across current references instead of reproducing the much longer archive of uncertain older codes.

## How to redeem Anime Guardians codes

1. Launch Anime Guardians from its official Roblox page.
2. Wait for the lobby and side menu to load.
3. Select **Codes** on the right side of the screen.
4. Paste one code into the text field.
5. Select **Redeem**.

Claim one entry at a time. Check the relevant balance after each redemption so you can identify which reward arrived before entering the next code.

## What the different rerolls change

Trait Rerolls, Stat Rerolls and Super Stat Rerolls are separate resources. A large quantity in one category cannot automatically be used in another, so preserve the reward wording in the table when planning an upgrade.

Green Spins Coins are also distinct from normal Coins and Gems. `PARAT2SBR` and `UPD28.5` belong to that update currency, while `NEWGUILDSHINYBOOST` is the broader bundle with Gems, Coins and Trait Rerolls.

A Limit Breaker is an item rather than spendable currency. `BASEDEFENSE` is therefore useful for a different progression step than a reroll or coin code.

## Why an Anime Guardians code may fail

**The code expired after an update.** Anime Guardians rotates a large number of update codes.

**The case or punctuation changed.** Keep the period in `UPD28.5` and preserve every capital letter.

**The server is behind.** Leave and join a newly started server after a patch.

**The account already claimed it.** Codes are normally limited to one redemption per account.
