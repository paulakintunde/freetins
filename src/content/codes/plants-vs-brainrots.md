## How to redeem Plants & Brainrots codes

1. Launch Plants & Brainrots from the official Roblox page.
2. Finish the short tutorial by buying and placing a seed.
3. Select **Shop** on the left.
4. Open **Rewards**, then **Codes**.
5. Paste the code and select **Claim**.

The casing is mixed: `STACKS` is uppercase, while `disguise`, `stone`, `frozen` and `based` are recorded in lowercase by the two sources used for this table.

## Items, potion and cash

The list contains three direct items, one Lucky Potion and one Cash reward. Disguise Glasses, Medusa's Head and the Frost Grenade are recorded as one item each. `based` reports 5,000 Cash, while `STACKS` reports one Lucky Potion.

Those rewards are not given an invented combat value. Item effects can change, and a Lucky Potion does not guarantee a particular plant.

## Why a code may fail

**The tutorial is incomplete.** Finish the opening seed placement first.

**The capitalization differs.** Copy the exact casing from the table.

**The server is old.** Rejoin the official experience after an update.

**The account already claimed it.** Each code is generally single-use.
