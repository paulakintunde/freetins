## How to redeem Devil Hunter codes

1. Launch Devil Hunter from its official Roblox page.
2. Complete the tutorial and progress until the phone is unlocked.
3. Join the official Roblox community if the code screen requires membership.
4. Press **N** to open the phone.
5. Open the **Codes** app, paste one code and press **Enter**.

The phone is part of progression rather than an immediately available lobby button. A new account may need to reach Rank 1 before the Codes app can be used.

## Why a Devil Hunter code may fail

**The phone is still locked.** Finish the tutorial and reach the required rank.

**The Roblox community requirement is unmet.** Join the developer's community and rejoin the experience.

**A punctuation mark is missing.** Keep the final `?` in `ISTHISANERASERDEVIL?`.

**The server predates the patch.** Join a fresh server after an update or shutdown.
