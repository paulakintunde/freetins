## The short version

Codes pay potions, trait rerolls and instant rolls, all of which feed the rolling loop that gets you cards.

The thing to internalise is the cycle: codes arrive in numbered families with each anime crossover update and die within about five days. This is one of the fastest code cycles covered on Freetins, which makes a check timestamp far more useful to you than a month in a headline.

## How to redeem codes in Anime Card Clash

1. Launch Anime Card Clash on Roblox.
2. Click the Codes button on the in game navigation on the left of the screen.
3. Paste the code into the Enter code here box.
4. Press Redeem.

Rewards land in your inventory rather than being applied automatically, so potions need activating before a rolling session.

## Why is my Anime Card Clash code not working?

**It expired.** With a five day working life, this is the default explanation for anything not published in the last few days.

**You have not hit the rerolls threshold.** The community tier codes are gated behind a large lifetime rerolls count. There is no way around it other than playing.

**You are on an old server.** New codes issued alongside an update sometimes only work on updated servers. Rejoining resolves it.

**You mistyped a number.** Numbered families differ by a single digit, and it is easy to claim the same one twice while believing you have claimed two.

## When do new Anime Card Clash codes come out?

With each crossover update, in a numbered family named after the licensed series that update adds. Two recent updates followed exactly that pattern. The developer also issues codes as compensation after maintenance and at player milestones.

Because the family names track announced crossovers, the next code name is often predictable from the update teaser. The numbers are not, so the Discord codes channel remains the fastest route.

## What to spend the rewards on

Instant rolls at very high volume are the most valuable item in the reward bundles, because they compress hours of rolling into a single action. Save them for a period when your luck potions are active rather than firing them the moment they arrive. Trait rerolls are the second priority, since traits rather than base cards decide how a deck performs at the top end.

## Frequently asked questions

### How long do Anime Card Clash codes last?

Days. The code family that shipped with one recent crossover update had expired within roughly five days of release, which is short even by Roblox standards. Redeem on the day you find a code rather than saving it for a session later in the week.

### Why are codes numbered?

Because the developer issues them in families named after the anime crossover in each update, numbered one upwards. If you see the first two members of a family, the rest almost certainly exist, and the Discord codes channel will have them before any tracker does.

### Do some codes need a rerolls threshold?

The community tier codes have carried a lifetime rerolls requirement, reported at around half a million. A new account cannot claim those regardless of how current the code is, which is why they appear to fail for beginners.

### What do the potions do?

Luck potions raise rare pull odds, roll speed potions cut the wait between rolls, boss chance and border chance potions change specific drop conditions, and instant rolls skip the animation entirely at very high volume. Together they are the difference between a casual and a serious session.

### Are codes uppercase?

The current families are published in block capitals. Enter them exactly as printed and copy rather than retype, particularly for the numbered variants where a single digit changes the code.

### Is there a tier list for the cards?

Rankings shift with every crossover update, since each one introduces a new set that reshuffles the meta. A tier list that is not dated and rechecked after each update will send you into the wrong merges.

### Where do new codes appear first?

The official Discord codes channel, and at player milestones or after maintenance as compensation.
