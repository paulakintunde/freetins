## The short version

Codes pay Cursed Crystals, Lumens and Clan Rolls, and the clan rolls are the reason to bother, since your clan shapes your build more than any other single roll.

One gate: the tutorial has to be finished before the Codes tab exists. Every report of a missing codes button traces back to that.

## How to redeem codes in Jujutsu Zero

1. Launch Jujutsu Zero on Roblox.
2. Move forward, speak to the NPC, equip the weapon from your bag and fight through the tutorial.
3. Once you land in the lobby, open the Codes tab from the main menu.
4. Type the code into the Enter Code Here box.
5. Press Redeem.

## Why is my Jujutsu Zero code not working?

**You skipped the tutorial.** No tutorial, no codes tab.

**You are trying a code from a different Jujutsu game.** Four similar titles, four separate code lists, and a great deal of cross posting between them on the wider web.

**It expired.** Update codes here move quickly, though milestone codes have proven more durable.

**You already claimed it.** One redemption per account.

## When do new Jujutsu Zero codes come out?

Two triggers. Updates, since the game is in active beta development with techniques and raids still being added, and like milestones, which arrive in ten thousand increments. The milestone codes are named after the threshold, so watching the like count on the experience page tells you both when a code is coming and roughly what it will be called.

## Frequently asked questions

### Why can I not see the codes option?

Because you have not finished the tutorial. The Codes tab appears in the main menu only after you complete the opening fight and return to the lobby. Players who skip ahead or disconnect during the tutorial report the button as missing, and this is why.

### What do the codes give?

Cursed Crystals, Lumens and Clan Rolls. Clan Rolls are the important one, since your clan determines a large part of your build and rerolling it otherwise costs time or Robux.

### Does Jujutsu Zero have a Trello or a wiki?

No, and that is unusual for a game this size. There has been discussion in the official Discord about creating them, but at present the information players need about clans, cursed techniques and weapons is scattered across Discord messages rather than documented anywhere central.

### Are milestone codes different from update codes?

In practice yes. This game's like milestone codes have tended to stay redeemable longer than its update codes, so an older milestone code is worth trying even when an update code of the same age has clearly gone.

### Is this the same game as Jujutsu Infinite?

No. Jujutsu Zero, Jujutsu Infinite, Jujutsu Shenanigans and Kaizen are four separate Roblox experiences inspired by the same series, and their codes are frequently published under the wrong title.

### How do I predict the next code?

Watch the like count on the Roblox experience page. Milestone codes are named after the threshold they celebrate and arrive in ten thousand increments, so the name of the next one is knowable before it is issued.

### Is the game finished?

No, it is in beta and the developer is still adding techniques, raids and rewards, which is why the code cadence tracks both patches and milestones.
