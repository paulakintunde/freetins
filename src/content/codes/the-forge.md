## What 15 Rerolls means

Rerolls provide attempts to change eligible build results. They do not guarantee a particular weapon, ore combination, rune or trait.

The game describes its core loop as mining, forging and fighting, with many possible forged variations. That makes a reroll useful, but it still has no fixed cash or real-money value that can be stated safely.
