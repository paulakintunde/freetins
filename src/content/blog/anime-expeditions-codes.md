## Redeem through the menu

1. Launch Anime Expeditions from its official listing.
2. Open the menu marked by dots at the top left.
3. Choose Redeem Codes and paste the complete string.
4. Select Redeem Code, then inspect the confirmation.

The alternative route is to interact with Razorjaw near spawn and choose Redeem Codes. Both reported paths reach the redemption interface. Walking to the NPC is useful if the menu is hard to find, but switching routes does not reset a used code.

## Spend crystals after choosing a unit

Trait resources create another opportunity to roll; they do not promise the trait you want. Decide which unit is worth keeping before using a limited supply on it. Check the trait panel's costs and the existing result so a reward does not turn into an accidental downgrade.

Stat and Equipment Rerolls are separately named resources in older bundles. Do not assume that a code advertised broadly as free rerolls fills every reroll balance. Compare the granted item with the control you intend to use.

## Read an error before retrying

Preserve the exclamation mark in restartsorry! and any other punctuation. Check for accidental spaces, an already-redeemed response or an eligibility message. Some historical promotions have been reported with level requirements; the context for a specific string matters more than a generic redeem guide.

The archive rows document what older promotions offered. Their presence is not a recommendation to keep submitting them after the game rejects them, and a copied list cannot establish a new reward window.
