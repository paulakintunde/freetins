## Redeem from the settings cog

1. Launch Bee Swarm Simulator from the official listing.
2. Open the settings cog at the top left.
3. Find the code field and enter the string.
4. Select Redeem and inspect the granted reward or effect.

For a club-gated entry, join the [official Bee Swarm Simulator Club](https://www.roblox.com/groups/3982592/) on the same account first. If a membership requirement is rejected, confirm you joined the correct group rather than assuming any bee-related community qualifies.

## Prepare before a timed boost

Select the field and equipment you intend to use before claiming a reward that activates a boost. Make room for the session, check your route and avoid spending the useful window deciding what to do next. The exact effect and timer should come from the game, not from a generic promise that all boosts behave alike.

Consumables are different from effects that begin on redemption. Inspect your inventory and the active-effect display to establish which kind you received. The name of the code does not determine when every item will be consumed.

## Tickets and treats do not guarantee a Mythic Bee

A Ticket reward is a resource grant. A consumable bundle provides the named items. Neither description establishes a guaranteed Mythic Bee or a free Robux payment. Keep the actual item names in view when comparing a code guide with a dramatic video headline.

Use the reward where it fits your hive's needs rather than spending everything simply because the claim was free. A code can add options; the choice of upgrade still depends on what you own and what you are trying to improve.
