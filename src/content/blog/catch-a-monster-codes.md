## Redeem through the gift menu

1. Launch the official experience linked in Publisher sources.
2. Select the gift icon at the top right.
3. Open Gift Code and paste the code into its field.
4. Choose Redeem and read the grant message.

Capitalization can matter. If a source prints BIGOPUS or HALLOWEEN2 in uppercase, paste that form first. The short xp and coin strings belong in Gift Code even though they resemble ordinary interface labels.

## Treat an egg or chest as a container

After a successful claim, look for the named egg or chest in the appropriate inventory. Follow the game's own instructions to use it. The claim step and the opening step can be separate, so the absence of a new monster immediately after redemption does not by itself prove a failure.

Do not infer a guaranteed hatch from a dramatic egg name. The table records the reported container reward only. A specific creature or drop rate needs its own publisher evidence.

## Read status labels as source reports

Active, confirmed, unconfirmed, check and expired are labels used by the cited sites. They do not mean that Freetins successfully redeemed a code. Where sources disagree, the table and disagreement notes retain both positions.

The clearest current overlap is BIGOPUS, STOWERBUG, COILRIFT, STELLAWOLF, coin and xp. Even that overlap can change without notice. Codes tied to Halloween or other events may close when the event ends, and the official page does not publish a fixed expiry schedule.

If a code fails, preserve the complete message. A mistyped string, prior claim and withdrawn promotion are different explanations. Retrying through the same menu can correct a typing mistake, but it cannot establish a new reward window.

## What we could not verify

Freetins did not redeem these codes, confirm the current event window or resolve the official page's unavailable label. The cited sources also disagree on the overall working list, on OBLIVION and ABYSSAL, and on the potion size granted by coin. Those limits remain visible instead of being converted into a successful redemption claim.
