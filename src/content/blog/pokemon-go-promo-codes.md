## Redeem through the Web Store

1. Open the official offer-redemption page linked under Publisher sources.
2. Sign in with a login method linked to the Trainer account you use in Pokémon GO.
3. Confirm the account before entering a code.
4. Submit the exact string and read the result.
5. Return to Pokémon GO and inspect the relevant research, avatar item or inventory.

If the login method you normally use is not offered, consult the game's account-linking help rather than creating a different Trainer and expecting the reward to transfer. A successful web login is only useful when it belongs to the intended game account.

## A research code does not instantly award the cap

The LEGO cap offer unlocks Timed Research. Complete the required tasks and claim their rewards in the game. The publisher's deadline applies to finishing and collecting, so redeeming shortly before the cutoff can still leave too little time to complete the work.

The publisher also identifies regions with alternative research instead of the cap offer. Read the research actually delivered to your account. A different regional reward is not necessarily a failed redemption, and no location-spoofing workaround is needed to explain the distinction.

## Keep the MLB offers separate

The globally available T-shirt code is distinct from ballpark activities and team cap rewards. You do not need to treat every detail of the baseball event as a prerequisite for the web code. Conversely, the T-shirt code is not a promise of every ballpark reward.

The publisher states a closing date for the T-shirt string without giving the exact code cutoff time in that announcement. The table preserves that precision rather than borrowing a time from a separate Timed Research activity.

## Handle older and disputed strings honestly

The FENDI entry has a conflict between the original window and a later community report of acceptance. The Worlds Tee and Mega Finale entries are historical reports. Those contexts help identify the offers without implying they all remain fresh.

A supply code should be checked against the relevant inventory, while an avatar reward belongs in customization and a research code belongs in the research view. Look in the right place before interpreting an unchanged item count as a failed claim.
