No row is starred yet; the reward table separates that claim from redeemable strings so you can find the gift without trying invented codes.

## Find the yellow gift

[BloxInformer's illustrated gift guide](https://bloxinformer.com/wikis/steal-an-egg/codes/) places a large yellow present beside the Most Money leaderboard near the base area. Walk into its green circle to open the reward prompt. Follow the prompt's requirements, then use Claim.

1. Open the official game from Publisher sources.
2. Like and favorite the experience if the gift prompt asks you to.
3. Join the linked and Collect Rare Pets community on Roblox.
4. Return to the gift prompt and claim when its requirements are satisfied.

Check the displayed reward before collecting it. The source reports Speed, not a guaranteed rare pet, an egg or Robux. If the prompt changes, its own wording is the decisive instruction for your account.

## If the gift will not unlock

Confirm that the group you joined belongs to this exact experience. Similar names make it easy to join an unrelated community. Re-entering the experience can help it notice a completed group requirement, but a retry does not establish that a changed or removed offer remains available.

Do not paste guessed words into chat or a purchase prompt. Neither is evidence of a code system. A missing redemption button is a reason to look for the documented gift, not to install a script that claims to add one.

## Where a future code announcement belongs

Start with the social links attached to the official Roblox listing and the publisher's community. A Discord notification role is not a released code. Wait for an actual developer announcement that includes a string, reward and redemption instructions before treating it as a new offer.
