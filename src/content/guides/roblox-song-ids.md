    a: No. A song ID only does something where the experience gives you a boombox, radio, car stereo or music panel that accepts a typed number. Plenty of experiences have no such item, and some that do restrict input to a list the owner has approved. An ID that works in one place can be refused in another without anything being wrong with the ID.
  - q: Why are my Roblox music codes not working?
    a: In most cases the audio behind the number is gone rather than the number being mistyped. Roblox scrubs uploads that draw copyright claims and replaces their titles, and the privacy rules set most older uploads private so nobody else's experience can load them. A smaller share of failures come from IDs that never pointed at audio at all, or from an experience that blocks typed input.

    a: You do not carry one between experiences. Each experience supplies its own. Some hand it to everyone in the backpack bar at the bottom of the screen, some sell it as a game pass through their shop button, and some limit it to private servers.   - q: How many digits is a Roblox audio ID?
    a: Older uploads carry nine or ten digits, and newer ones carry up to fifteen. Length alone tells you nothing about whether an ID still works.     a: They are the same kind of thing. ## How to use a Roblox song ID in game

1. Join an experience that supplies a boombox, radio, car stereo or music panel. You cannot bring one in from outside.
2. Claim or buy the item. Free ones appear on their own. Paid ones sit behind the experience's own shop or store button, usually as a game pass.
3. Look at the hotbar along the bottom of the screen. The boombox occupies a numbered slot there once you own it.
4. Click the slot, press the matching number key, or tap the slot on a touchscreen. Your avatar picks the boombox up.
5. Click or tap the equipped boombox. A single input box opens.
6. Type or paste the digits only. No `rbxassetid://` prefix, no spaces, no quotation marks.
7. Press Enter or the play button. Audio loads over the network, so a short delay before the first note is normal.

If nothing plays and the input box clears itself, the experience rejected the asset rather than the number. That is the same failure mode covered in [redeeming a game code without losing it to a typo](/blog/how-to-redeem-game-codes/).

## Why Roblox song IDs stop working

Ordered by how often each cause turned up in the sample of published IDs tested for this page.

1. **Roblox scrubbed the upload after a copyright claim.** The asset still exists but its title has been replaced with a takedown notice, and the file is no longer served. This is the single largest cause.
2. **The upload is private.** Roblox's privacy rules set most older audio private, so an experience that does not own the asset cannot load it. The asset page still shows the original title, which is why these IDs look alive from a distance.
3. **The upload was deleted outright.** Roblox returns a content deleted marker in place of the title.
4. **The number never pointed at audio.** Some widely republished IDs resolve to a place or an image asset. A boombox will never play those, no matter how many lists carry them.
5. **The asset record is gone entirely.** Roblox returns nothing at all for the number.
6. **The experience blocked it.** The ID is fine, and the owner has restricted typed input or the audio itself.

## How to check a Roblox song ID yourself in under a minute

1. Open `https://create.roblox.com/store/asset/` followed by the digits.
2. Read the title. A live track shows its real name and artist. A scrubbed one shows a removed for copyright marker or a content deleted marker in place of the title.
3. For a second opinion, open `https://economy.roblox.com/v2/assets/` followed by the digits and then `/details`. It returns plain text in the browser. Look for `AssetTypeId` of 3, which means audio. Anything else is not a song.
4. To test public availability, open `https://assetdelivery.roblox.com/v2/assetId/` followed by the digits. A public file returns a location. A restricted one returns an authentication message.
