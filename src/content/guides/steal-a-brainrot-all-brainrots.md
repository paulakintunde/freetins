## How many brainrots are in Steal a Brainrot?

There is no published answer. The community index counts 513 entries, of which 499 are indexable in game. One outlet says more than 525, another says more than 200, and a third says more than 150. Those four numbers cannot all be right, and none of them comes from {{developer}}.

So this page does the opposite. It records only brainrots it has documented one at a time, and the total above is generated from the table rather than typed. The Common, Rare, Epic, Legendary, Mythic and OG tiers are recorded in full. The Brainrot God and Secret tiers are recorded in part, because those two tiers are larger than any published figure agrees on and padding them would mean guessing.

## How do you get brainrots in Steal a Brainrot?

1. Wait at the Red Carpet. Brainrots walk from the cave along the carpet and you buy one as it passes. This is the only route that covers every rarity class.
2. Accept that spawning is weighted, not random. The game files hold a weight per brainrot rather than a percentage, and the highest weight in the game belongs to the cheapest Common. A higher weight means it appears more often, nothing more.
3. Listen for the announcement. A Brainrot God or a Secret spawn is called out in your server, and an OG spawn is announced globally on the live spawns panel by the entrance.
4. Outbid or defend. Another player can buy a brainrot already heading to your base for 150 percent of its base cost, which redirects it to them.
5. Steal from other bases. This is the mechanic the experience is named after and it is how most high tier brainrots change hands.
6. Open Lucky Blocks and run machines. Fuse and craft machines produce brainrots that never appear on the carpet, and the current one is the Bee Lucky Block.
7. Watch for [what admins can hand out during an Admin Abuse window](/guides/steal-a-brainrot-admin-abuse/). Several entries in the table exist only because of those events and cannot be farmed at all.
8. Check the status column before you grind. Rows marked Expired have had their route retired, so no amount of play will produce one.
