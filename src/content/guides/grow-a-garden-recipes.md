  - q: How do you make ice cream in Grow a Garden?
    a: Put one cone crop and one sweet crop into the Cooking Kit and press Cook. Corn plus Blueberry is the cheapest pair that works, and Cauliflower, Banana, Violet Corn or Sugarglaze all fill the cone slot instead.   - q: How do you make sushi in Grow a Garden?
    a: Every recorded sushi recipe contains Bamboo and a corn family crop. Four Bamboo plus one Corn is the combination two outlets and the wiki all give.   - q: Why did my cooking pot make Soup?
    a: Soup is the fallback output for any combination the game does not recognise as another food. It is not a recipe you can look up, and there is nothing hidden to discover.   - q: Do Chris P. cravings still work?
    a: No. Chris P. and the craving system left with the Cooking Event, and the Cooking Kit replaced the lobby pot. Any recipe still labelled craving only, or that promises a free one tier upgrade, is describing a mechanic that is no longer in the experience. That is the main reason older recipe lists fail when people follow them.
  - q: How do you unlock cooking in Grow a Garden?
    a: You need the Cooking Kit, which is claimed from the achievement sets in the Garden Guide. The Common set is the quickest route, and it asks for ordinary planting, harvesting, shovelling, selling and gifting tasks. Once claimed, the kit appears in your cosmetics inventory and you place it anywhere on your plot.

## Every Grow a Garden recipe

{{table:recipes|status=active,unverified}}

Recipes in this game are slot based, not fixed lists. Each food declares a set of slots, and each slot accepts any crop from one group. Ice Cream declares a cone slot and a sweet slot, so any cone crop plus any sweet crop produces Ice Cream. Extra crops beyond the required slots add weight and rarity, they do not change which food comes out.

That is why so many published lists contradict each other. They record one worked combination and present it as the recipe, when the game only ever checked the slots. The worked examples above are the cheapest combination two independent sources agree on. Use the crop groups if you want to substitute, and check [which crops count as sweet, fruit and leafy](/guides/grow-a-garden-plant-types/) before you commit a rare seed to the pot.

Nothing here displays as Active. Grow a Garden does not publish recipes on any channel it controls, and a row cannot be marked Active without publisher confirmation. Every row is therefore Unverified, which is the honest label, and [how every row on this page is checked](/how-we-verify/) explains what that status is worth.

## How to make ice cream in Grow a Garden

1. Grow or buy one cone crop: Cauliflower, Corn, Banana, Violet Corn or Sugarglaze.
2. Grow or buy one sweet crop. Blueberry and Strawberry are the cheapest.
3. Walk up to your placed Cooking Kit holding the first crop and submit it.
4. Submit the second crop. Both appear on the ingredient board.
5. Check the timer box, which reads 5 minutes at base weight.
6. Press the green Cook button and wait for the timer to clear.

{{table:recipes|classification=Ice Cream}}

Adding a second sweet crop turns the same pot into a Donut, because Donut declares two sweet slots. If you want Ice Cream, submit exactly one sweet crop.

## How to make sushi in Grow a Garden

1. Wait for Bamboo to appear in the Seed Shop rotation and buy several.
2. Grow the Bamboo to full size and harvest it.
3. Submit four Bamboo to the Cooking Kit, one at a time.
4. Submit one Corn or Violet Corn as the rice slot.
5. Press Cook and wait out the 5 minute base timer.

{{table:recipes|classification=Sushi}}

Bamboo is the hard requirement. Every recorded sushi recipe contains it, so no substitution exists for that slot. Sources agree up to Divine and split above it.

## How to make pizza in Grow a Garden

1. Submit one bread crop such as Corn or Cauliflower.
2. Submit one meat crop such as Pepper, Cactus or Mushroom.
3. Submit one vegetable crop such as Tomato.
4. Submit one fruit crop such as Strawberry for the sauce.
5. Press Cook. Pizza carries the longest base timer of any food at 8 minutes.

## How to make cake in Grow a Garden

1. Submit one bread crop.
2. Submit one sweet crop for the icing slot.
3. Submit a second sweet crop for the filling slot.
4. Submit one fruit crop.
5. Press Cook and wait out the 6 minute base timer.

{{table:recipes|classification=Cake}}

Cake is the one row on this page flagged as conflicting. The declared slots ask for four crop types, but the combination the community reports uses two. Follow the slots if the community version gives you something else.

## How to make a donut in Grow a Garden

1. Submit one bread crop such as Corn.
2. Submit one sweet crop for the icing slot.
3. Submit a second, different sweet crop for the sprinkles slot.
4. Press Cook. Donut has one of the shortest base timers at 3 minutes.

## How to make salad in Grow a Garden

1. Submit one leafy crop such as Tomato, Artichoke or Onion.
2. Submit one vegetable crop. Two Tomato will satisfy both slots.
3. Press Cook and wait out the 6 minute base timer.

{{table:recipes|classification=Salad}}

Salad has the lowest base weight of any food at 0.2kg, so weight driven rarity is hardest to earn here. Cook something heavier if rarity is what you are chasing.

## How the cooking mechanic works

1. Claim the Cooking Kit from an achievement set in the Garden Guide.
2. Open your cosmetics inventory and place the kit anywhere on your plot.
3. Hold a harvested crop and walk into the kit to submit it as an ingredient.
4. Watch the ingredient board fill as each crop takes a slot.
5. Watch the timer box, which rises with the weight and number of crops you add.
6. Watch the mutation gauge, which shows the chance of a crop mutation carrying into the food.
7. Press the green Cook button to start the timer.
8. Wait for the timer, or pay 39 Robux to skip it.
9. Collect the finished food and use it for crafting, pet hunger, the Sell Stand or an NPC birthday.

Weight is the lever most people miss. A heavier crop adds time to the pot and raises the grade of the finished food, so a giant crop is worth more in the pot than a second ordinary one. If you are short of crops entirely, the [current Grow a Garden codes](/codes/grow-a-garden/) are the fastest way to top up seeds before you start.
