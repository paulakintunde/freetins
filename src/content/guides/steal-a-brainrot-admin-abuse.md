## What actually happens, and what you can get

The host applies effects across every server at once rather than visiting them one at a time. In practice that means luck multipliers switched on for the whole game, raised spawn rates for the top rarity tiers, mutation and trait weather, and lucky blocks dropped in bulk. Themed machines appear next to the shop and convert brainrots you already own into ones you cannot otherwise get.

The payload column in the tables above is the concrete version of that. It is where the exclusive machines, the limited stock brainrots and the trait multipliers actually landed. If you want to know what a given spawn is worth holding, [the full brainrot roster with rarity tiers](/guides/steal-a-brainrot-all-brainrots/) lists them with their income figures.

Two practical points that repeat across sessions. Clear base slots before it starts, because you cannot collect what you have no room for. And sit in a private server, because the same effects apply there and nobody can rob you while you are picking things up.

## What to do if you miss it

Most of what a session spawns stays in the economy afterwards, so the normal fix is trading rather than waiting for the next one. Limited stock items and event exclusive machines are the exception, and those genuinely close. Before you chase something, check whether it is still obtainable at all.

Nothing given out during a session is gated behind a code, so [current Steal a Brainrot codes](/blog/steal-a-brainrot-codes/) are a separate route to rewards and are worth redeeming regardless. And if you want to work out what a missed spawn is actually worth to you, [check the roster for its rarity and income](/guides/steal-a-brainrot-all-brainrots/) before paying a trading price for it.
