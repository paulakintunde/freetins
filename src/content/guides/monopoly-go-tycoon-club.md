## What the Tycoon Club actually is

{{developer}} describes it as the game's loyalty programme: purchases made through the club are rewarded with added value and with **Loyalty Points**, which you then spend in a Benefits section for bundles that arrive in your game.

That is the whole mechanism. The club is a second shop with a points scheme bolted on. It does not hand out dice for playing, it does not replace the in-game store, and the points it issues cannot be earned by playing at all.

## Is the Monopoly GO Tycoon Club free?

Joining is free. {{developer}} lists activity thresholds for the invitation and names no purchase requirement anywhere in its own documentation.

What sits inside splits cleanly in two. **The free half is the Store free gift, the profile card, the achievement badges and your own play statistics.** The paid half is everything driven by Loyalty Points, and Loyalty Points only exist because somebody spent money in the web Store.

## How to open the Tycoon Club, step by step

The route depends on whether the in-game button is showing for you. Both routes end in the same place.

### If the button is in your game

1. Open MONOPOLY GO! on the device your account is signed in on.
2. Tap the menu icon in the top corner of the board screen.
3. Select **Tycoon Club** from the menu.
4. Copy the link from the popup that appears. The popup exists because the club will not open inside the app.
5. Paste the link into your device's browser and open it.

### If the button is missing

1. Open your phone's browser. Do this on the phone, not a desktop.
2. Go to the official MONOPOLY GO! site. {{developer}} names the site root, and nothing deeper.
3. Tap **LOGIN** in the top corner.
4. Sign in with the Facebook account linked to your game.
5. Open the club from the menu once you are signed in.

Rewards and purchases from the club are pushed into the game automatically. There is nothing to claim on the app side beyond opening it.

### Keeping it one tap away

This is where the two platforms genuinely differ, and where most guides stop short.

1. **Android:** sign in through your browser, tap **Add** when the prompt appears, then confirm the shortcut.
2. **iOS:** open the club in Safari, tap the **Share** button at the bottom of the screen, choose **Add to Home Screen**, then tap **Add**.

Devices running iOS 16 or earlier may fail to stay signed in through that home screen shortcut. {{developer}}'s own answer is to update the device. Until you do, use Safari directly.

## Why can't I see the Tycoon Club button?

{{table:issues}}

The missing button is the reason most people arrive here. It is not a sign your membership lapsed. **{{developer}} runs a standing banner across its help centre telling players who cannot see the button in game to reach the club through a browser instead**, which is both the workaround and the clearest evidence that the underlying fault is still open.

One knock-on effect is worth knowing before you spend. Web purchases are processed by Xsolla rather than Apple or Google, so a refund request does not go through your app store. The receipt lives in your Xsolla account and the request goes to {{developer}} support.

### What resets, and when

{{table:benefits|classification=Time-limited}}

The milestone track is the trap. Points stored in it reset to zero the moment a new track begins, and a three day break sits between one Tycoon Pass and the next. Banking Loyalty Points across a season change loses them. The reward list inside any given track is season specific, which is why no list of track rewards is published on this page: naming last season's rewards as current is the single most common failure across the pages ranking for this term.
