faq:
  - q: How many entries are in Pipeline Check?
    a: The roster holds every entry recorded on this page, and each row carries its own state and the date an editor last checked it, so you can tell a verified entry from one that is still awaiting editor verification at a glance. Entries that leave the game are retired by an editor and stay in the table as Expired rather than being deleted.
  - q: What is the rarest entry?
    a: Rarity is shown as its own column in the master table, and the rarity sub-tables further down group every entry by that classification. Where a rarity is derived from community observation rather than stated in the game itself, the notes on that row say so plainly.
  - q: Why do two sources give different income figures for one entry?
    a: Two independent outlets report different income figures for it and no publisher channel states a number. Rather than pick a side or average them, the row stays in the table, listed as awaiting editor verification, and the disagreement is shown in full further down the page so you can judge it yourself until an editor settles it in the game.
  - q: Do retired entries ever come back?
    a: Nothing on the current record suggests a retired entry has returned. An entry an editor retires stays in the table as Expired because people continue to search for it, and because keeping it is the clearest proof that this page is actually maintained rather than left to rot. If one returns, an editor records that as a dated event.
  - q: How often is this page rechecked?
    a: Rechecks are recorded on the page itself as editors make them, and no schedule is promised because none would be honest. The change log shows the last five real changes, so a recheck that found nothing new is recorded as it happened instead of appearing as a silent date bump, and a row nobody has tested says so in its Last checked column.
  - q: Where do new entries get announced first?
    a: The publisher's own channels are listed in the sources section at the foot of the page. Those are the origin for every fact here, and they are where a change shows up before any aggregator or outlet picks it up and republishes it.
