# Verification checklist: Grow a Garden recipes

**Entity:** Grow a Garden, place ID 126884695634066, by The Garden Game
**Entity URL:** https://www.roblox.com/games/126884695634066/Grow-a-Garden
**Disambiguation:** not Grow a Garden 2 (place ID 97598239454123, Strawberreh Squad)

## Access path

1. Join the experience at the entity URL above. No account level or pass is needed.
2. Open your inventory and search for "Garden Guide". Click to open it.
3. Open the Achievements panel and claim the Cooking Kit from the Common set.
4. Open the cosmetics inventory (hammer and spanner icon, top left) and place the Cooking Kit on your plot.
5. Harvest the crops named in each line below, hold one, and walk into the kit to submit it.
6. Read the ingredient board (left) and the timer box (right) before pressing Cook.

## Prerequisites per row

- Bamboo, Crown Melon, Pumpkin, Coconut, Sugarglaze, Giant Pinecone, Rose and Peach are Seed Shop rotation items. Check stock before starting the Sushi, Pie, Waffle, Corndog and Sweet Tea lines.
- Every other line uses starter or common shop crops.
