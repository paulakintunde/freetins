## Access path a tester follows

1. Open https://www.roblox.com/games/109983668079237/Steal-a-Brainrot and confirm the creator reads BRAZILIAN SPYDER with a verified badge. Do not test in Steal The Brainrot Base, Stealing Brainrots (Fortnite) or any browser clone.
2. Join and wait until you have spawned into your own base. Place 96342491571673 (New Players Servers) is the same universe and is also valid for testing.
3. Press the **Codes** button on the button stack down the left side of the screen. It is not in the shop menu.
4. Type the string into the field labelled **Code Here**. Type, do not paste.
5. Press **Submit**, then watch the red carpet.

## Prerequisites before testing

- No level or rebirth gate exists on the redemption field.
- Cash gate on rewards: a Secret tier spawn can cost around 80M and appears once. Bank the cash and stand at the red carpet tunnel entrance before pressing Submit, or another player takes it.
- One redemption per Roblox account per string. Use a fresh account if a row has already been tested.
- Stock caps, not dates, kill these codes. Record "sold out" separately from "invalid" if the game distinguishes them.
